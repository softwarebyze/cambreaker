CAMBREAKER — APP REVIEW NOTES (also pasted in Resolution Center reply)

1. PURPOSE & AUDIENCE
CamBreaker is a free, offline, single-player arcade game: a faithful recreation
of the classic BlackBerry-era brick-breaker, rebuilt for iPhone. Target audience
is casual mobile gamers and nostalgia players. There are no accounts, no ads,
no purchases, no network calls, and no data collection of any kind. The entire
game (34 boards, 3 lives, score, high scores stored on-device) works fully offline.

2. MAIN FEATURES & HOW TO ACCESS THEM (no login, no setup needed)
- PLAY: tap Play on the menu. Tap the screen to launch the ball; drag anywhere
  to slide the paddle (Touch steering, works on every device).
- CAMERA CONTROL STEERING (requires iPhone 16 or newer hardware): light-press
  the Camera Control button to open the system overlay, double-light-press to
  switch controls if needed, choose "Paddle", then swipe on the control to
  steer. A full press acts as the fire/launch button. A built-in "Camera Control
  test" screen on the menu demonstrates the slider live, and the pause menu
  shows the live swipe value.
- If the review device has no Camera Control button, everything is fully
  playable with Touch steering — no feature is locked behind the hardware.
- HOW TO PLAY screen documents all 10 capsules, scoring, and rules in-app.
- The attached screen recording (physical iPhone 17 Pro, latest iOS) shows:
  app launch, Play, ball launch, paddle steering, brick breaking.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES / TOOLS / PLATFORMS
None. The app makes zero network requests and integrates no third-party SDKs,
analytics, ads, auth, payments, or AI services. Camera frames are processed
on-device only for the live background/preview and are never stored or sent.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions identically in all regions and languages (English UI).

5. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. All artwork, boards, code, and sounds are original and created
for this app. "BlackBerry-style" in the description refers only to genre
homage (breakout gameplay); no BlackBerry trademarks, assets, or code are used.
